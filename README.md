Cycling Coach AI is a Python-based program designed to help cyclists plan and manage their training sessions. The program allows cyclists to describe how they feel on a particular day, such as feeling energetic, tired, sore, or ready for a hard workout. Based on the cyclist’s response, the AI can suggest an appropriate training session for that day.

The program can also help cyclists organize their training for the entire week by providing different types of workouts, such as endurance rides, interval training, recovery rides, sprint workouts, and rest days. This allows cyclists to have a more structured training routine instead of randomly choosing what workout to do.

Cycling Coach AI is designed to make training more personalized and easier to understand. By considering how the cyclist feels, the program can recommend a session that matches their current condition and training goals. It can help cyclists stay consistent, understand their training schedule, and work toward improving their cycling performance over time.

Overall, Cycling Coach AI aims to act as a simple digital cycling coach that helps cyclists decide what type of training session to do today or during the week based on how they feel and what they want to improve.
